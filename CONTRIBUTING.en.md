# How to add an experiment

Three requirements. They aren't arbitrary — they are [Article 4, 5, and 6 of the charter](CHARTER.en.md), put into practice.

## 1. One person can do it at home

- Budget ≤ ¥200 (~$30)
- Time ≤ one weekend (slow experiments may span a few days, but not "check every hour for a month")
- No professional facility, no license, no permit

## 2. Say what counts as success

Before you start, write it down: "if the number lands between 2.4 and 3.4 × 10⁸, it worked."

Not "look at the effect" or "feel it out" — that's not an experiment.

This is [Article 6 of the charter](CHARTER.en.md) in practice: **everyone has the right to submit evidence, but not everyone is right.**

## 3. Name the traps and the risks

Including "don't do this one."

Half the value of a good experiment page lives in its "traps" section.

---

## How to submit

1. Copy [experiments/_template.md](experiments/_template.md)
2. Save it as `experiments/<your-experiment>.md` (lowercase-hyphenated English, e.g. `microwave-speed-of-light.md`)
3. Add one row to the matching category table in [README.md](README.md) (and [README.en.md](README.en.md) if you can)
4. Open a PR

One experiment per PR. To edit someone else's page, just open a PR.

## Translations

Translations are welcome, one file per PR:

- Experiment page → `experiments/<name>.en.md` (or `.zh.md` if you're translating from English)
- Lists and charter → edit `README.en.md` / `CHARTER.en.md` / `MANIFESTO.en.md` directly

No need to wait for approval, no need to translate the whole batch. One page at a time.

## Not accepted

- Pseudo-science, mysticism, words like "quantum" or "energy" used to sell
- Helping a brand verify its product
- Health or treatment claims
- Dangerous work: high voltage, open flame, ingestion or inhalation of chemicals, live animals, anything that can hurt someone
- Link-only entries with no write-up

## Where to post your result

Add a line to [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md). Failures count more than successes.
